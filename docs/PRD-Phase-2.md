# Phase 2 - Core Product

## Goal

Make the app feel dependable and sticky for daily shared household use beyond the initial MVP.

## Deliverables

- Refine the home screen and chore status views for faster scanning
- Add stronger reminder controls and notification preferences
- Introduce a simple completion history view per chore
- Add better household setup flows for couples, roommates, or small families
- Expand gamification with lightweight streaks, points, or completion summaries
- Improve category management and filtering
- Polish installable app behavior and reliability

## Why This Phase Exists

Phase 1 proves that recurring chores, completion tracking, and polite bumps solve the core household problem. Phase 2 turns the MVP into a more habit-forming product by improving retention, usability, and clarity.

## Risks

- Too much gamification could distract from the app's practical utility.
- More reminder options could increase product complexity quickly.
- Supporting more than two users may require revisiting assumptions made in the MVP.

## Open Questions

- Which engagement features feel motivating rather than childish?
- Does the app need stronger analytics or summaries for household behavior?
- Should small-family use cases be officially supported here or deferred?
